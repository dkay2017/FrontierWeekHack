# Bupa / Comprehensive — Prior Authorisation Policy
## MRI of the lumbar spine (outpatient)
Policy reference: **bupa-mri-ls-v3** · CCSD procedure code: CCSD-72148 · Effective: 2026-01-01

Prior authorisation is **required** for outpatient MRI of the lumbar spine on the
Comprehensive plan.

### Approval criteria

| # | Criterion | Type |
|---|---|---|
| c1 | **Conservative treatment**: a documented course of physiotherapy completed for at least six weeks, with the outcome recorded. | **Mandatory** |
| c2 | **Neurological findings**: radiculopathy documented on clinical examination (a dermatomal sensory or motor deficit, or a reduced reflex). | Supporting |
| c3 | **Impact on management**: the imaging result would change clinical management — for example, determining whether to proceed to surgical decompression. | Supporting |

A request meets the policy when **c1 is documented** and **at least one** of c2 / c3
is documented. Where c1 is not evidenced the request is declined; where c1 is met
but c2 and c3 are absent the request is returned to the clinician to strengthen
the record.

### Denial reason codes

- **MN-01 — conservative treatment not evidenced**: the clinical note does not
  record a completed course of physiotherapy of adequate duration.
- **MN-02 — imaging not shown to change management**: the record does not
  establish that the scan would alter the treatment plan.

### Appeals

**Clause 2.1** — *Reconsideration on the documented record.* Where a denied
request is resubmitted with the clinical record that satisfies c1 and at least
one supporting criterion, and the payer's own recorded outcomes for a comparable
fact pattern support authorisation, the denial is overturned on reconsideration.
An appeal under clause 2.1 must cite the criteria met and the comparable cases
relied on.

Internal reconsideration is followed, if unresolved, by the Financial Ombudsman
Service.

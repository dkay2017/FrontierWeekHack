# Precedent P-1 — MRI lumbar spine — Bupa / Comprehensive (UK)
Decided on appeal: 2026-03-01 · Outcome: **appeal won**

## Fact pattern
A 44-year-old with eight weeks of completed physiotherapy and limited benefit.
Radiculopathy documented on examination in an L5 distribution with a reduced ankle
reflex. The requesting consultant recorded that the MRI findings would determine
whether to proceed to decompression surgery.

## History
Initially denied under **MN-01** (conservative treatment not evidenced) — the
first submission had not attached the physiotherapy discharge summary. Resubmitted
with the summary and the examination findings.

## Outcome
Authorised on reconsideration under **clause 2.1**: c1 (physiotherapy) and both
supporting criteria were satisfied on the documented record.

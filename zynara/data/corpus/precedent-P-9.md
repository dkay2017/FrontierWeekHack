# Precedent P-9 — MRI lumbar spine — Bupa / Comprehensive (UK)
Decided on appeal: 2026-02-01 · Outcome: **appeal lost**

## Fact pattern
Physiotherapy completed and radiculopathy noted, but the record did not explain
how the scan would change management — no surgical question was posed.

## History
Denied **MN-02** (imaging not shown to change management). Appealed on the basis
of the physiotherapy and examination findings alone.

## Outcome
Appeal unsuccessful. c1 and c2 were met but c3 was not, and the reviewer held that
clause 2.1 requires at least one supporting criterion to be genuinely evidenced —
the "impact on management" statement was absent.

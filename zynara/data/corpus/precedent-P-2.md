# Precedent P-2 — MRI lumbar spine — Bupa / Comprehensive (UK)
Decided on appeal: 2026-04-01 · Outcome: **appeal won**

## Fact pattern
Six weeks of physiotherapy completed. Radiculopathy on examination. MRI requested
to plan the surgical approach for a suspected disc prolapse.

## History
Denied **MN-01**. The clinical note clearly recorded the physiotherapy course and
the neurological deficit; the payer's reviewer had missed the examination findings.

## Outcome
Overturned on appeal citing **clause 2.1** and the documented neurological deficit.

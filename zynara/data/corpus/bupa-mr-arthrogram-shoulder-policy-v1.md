# Bupa / Comprehensive — Prior Authorisation Policy
## MR arthrogram of the shoulder (outpatient)
Policy reference: **bupa-mras-v1** · CCSD procedure code: CCSD-73222 · Effective: 2026-01-01

Prior authorisation is **required** for MR arthrogram of the shoulder.

### Approval criteria

| # | Criterion | Type |
|---|---|---|
| c1 | Conservative management including a course of physiotherapy has been attempted. | **Mandatory** |
| c2 | Instability or a labral tear is suspected clinically (e.g. positive apprehension test). | Supporting |
| c3 | Plain radiographs of the shoulder have already been obtained and reviewed. | Supporting |

This is a lower-volume procedure with limited appeal history on record; borderline
requests are routed to a senior reviewer.
